360Zone

A study workspace with writing, grammar, citation, evidence, and math tools.

Overview

360Zone is the study-focused app in the 360 collection.

Writing Tools

Available tools include Paragraph Builder, Grammar Fixer, MLA Generator, Evidence Helper, and Pronoun Checker. Results can be copied from the tool panels.

Math Tools

The math section includes a calculator, equation solver, and word problem helper.

Classroom

The Classroom area provides a place to use the study tools together. The app also includes a dark mode control.
