360Meet

Browser-based meetings with account controls and meeting permissions.

Overview

360Meet is the meeting and calling app in the 360 collection.

Meetings

The app provides a meeting interface with controls for joining and leaving a meeting.

Permissions

Browser permissions can be requested for meeting features. The interface also includes controls for managing participants and screen sharing.

Account

Sign in, sign up, and sign out are available from the account menu.
