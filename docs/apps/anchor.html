Anchor

Site management tools for domains, crawling, DNS verification, indexing, and sitemaps.

Overview

Anchor is the site management and search indexing tool in the 360 app collection.

Sites

Signed-in users can add and manage sites from the My Sites area.

Verification

Domain ownership can be verified with a DNS TXT record or an HTML meta tag.

Indexing

Anchor provides controls for requesting indexing and submitting a sitemap.

Coverage

The dashboard includes coverage information and a list of recently crawled pages.
